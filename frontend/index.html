The Intelligence Loop

From city hall to your phone in under 24 hours

Step 01

Permit Filed at City Hall

A new restaurant, clinic, or retail business signs a commercial lease and files a building permit to begin interior construction. This creates an official government record — public, legally required, and time-stamped.

Step 02

We Index, Clean, and Structure It

Our pipeline queries official Socrata municipal endpoints every weekday morning, normalizes the raw government filings, and structures each record with jobsite address, declared budget, contractor of record, and full scope of work.

Step 03

You Arrive at the Jobsite First

Your member portal updates overnight. You export to CSV, plan your territory route, and walk into the active construction site 60 to 90 days before your competitors know that business exists.
